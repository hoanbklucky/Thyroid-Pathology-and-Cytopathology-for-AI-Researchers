# Online thyroid pathology and cytopathology image resources

Curated external links for visual learning. Prefer **viewing images on the original host websites**; respect copyright, access restrictions, and restrictions on AI training or redistribution.

| Resource | What it offers | Suggested use |
|---|---|---|
| [NUS CytoWeb thyroid picture atlas](https://blog.nus.edu.sg/cytoweb/picture-atlas/thyroid/) | Thyroid cytology image collection (user-recommended) | Review cellular patterns and nuclear features |
| [NUS Pathweb: FNA thyroid picture atlas](https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/pathweb/cytopath/picture-atlas/fna-thyroid/) | Annotated cases grouped by Bethesda-style headings: colloid nodules, thyroiditis, AUS, follicular neoplasm, PTC, medullary and anaplastic carcinoma | Best first case-by-case comparison |
| [Papanicolaou Society: thyroid web atlas](https://www.papsociety.org/atlas/) | Hundreds of cytology images, including rare entities, searchable by diagnostic category | Explore the full Bethesda spectrum; note atlas images are older and not a substitute for 2023 Bethesda criteria |
| [Papanicolaou Society: benign category](https://papsociety.org/atlas/index.php?cat=3) | Benign follicular nodules, Hashimoto and granulomatous thyroiditis | Build benign morphological vocabulary |
| [Papanicolaou Society: suspicious category](https://www.papsociety.org/atlas/index.php?cat=6) | Suspicious PTC, medullary carcinoma, metastasis, lymphoma | Understand borderline/suspicious cytology |
| [NUS Pathweb: quick approaches, cases, quizzes](https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/pathweb/cytopath/quick-approaches-cases/) | Thyroid overview (2026 update), oncocytic lesions, NIFTP cyto-histologic correlation, MTC/ATC cases and quizzes | Guided self-assessment after Modules 2–3 |
| [NUS Pathweb: endocrine pathology slide catalogue](https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/pathweb/catalogue_videos/) | Gross and microscopic thyroid specimens, including follicular adenoma, PTC and MTC | Learn histology that cannot be assessed from FNA alone |
| [NUS Pathweb: thyroid diagnostic challenges](https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/pathweb/virtual-pathology-museum/test-your-diagnostic-skills-thyroid-and-endocrine-system/) | Thyroid gross pathology cases with clinical context | Test architecture and entity recognition |

## Recommended order
1. NUS Pathweb FNA atlas: benign → AUS → follicular neoplasm → PTC → MTC → ATC.
2. Papanicolaou Society: browse the same categories, including preparation/stain variations.
3. NUS Pathweb quick cases and quizzes: attempt diagnosis before revealing annotations.
4. Compare FNA images with NUS histology slides; explicitly note which features require tissue architecture.

## Image use and provenance
- **NUS Pathweb** explicitly allows attributed links to whole webpages but restricts copying, downloading, redistributing, or reusing media without permission; do not mirror its images into this repo or an AI training dataset.
- **Papanicolaou Society** states its atlas images may be used for educational purposes with attribution. This does **not** automatically authorize model training, commercial use, or redistribution under an open-data license.
- Record the source URL, stain, specimen preparation, diagnosis, and licensing terms for any image used in an annotation exercise.

For tumor terminology and the distinction between Bethesda cytology and final histology, see [Thyroid lesions and Bethesda](THYROID_LESIONS_AND_BETHESDA.md).
