# Module 1 — Tissue architecture

## Concepts
- **Follicle:** epithelial cell layer enclosing a lumen, typically containing colloid.
- **Follicular pattern:** tumor cells organized in follicle-like spaces (macrofollicles or microfollicles).
- **Papillary pattern:** branching epithelial projections with fibrovascular cores (true papillae).
- **Neoplasm:** abnormal clonal proliferation; can be benign, low risk, or malignant.
- **Capsule:** fibrous boundary around some tumors; **capsular invasion** is tumor growth through the capsule.
- **Vascular invasion:** tumor present within qualifying blood vessels; requires careful histologic evaluation.
