# Module 2 — Nuclear morphology and cytology

## Visual vocabulary
- Nuclear enlargement and elongation
- Nuclear crowding/overlap
- Chromatin clearing / ground-glass appearance
- Nuclear membrane irregularity and grooves
- Intranuclear cytoplasmic pseudoinclusions

**Important:** No single isolated feature should be treated as an autonomous cancer test. Features are interpreted in context and can be subtle or heterogeneous.

## Read the supplied Ohori et al. (2026) paper
- Page 6, Table 2 and Figure 1: classical papillary versus papillary-like nuclei.
- Page 7, Figure 2: conceptual nuclear scoring and overlap.
- Page 8, Figure 3: cytology examples, especially relevant to FNA images.
